1. Append-only at scale

CPU breaks first, not memory. After every request the ledger re-checks every closed day of every account for a missing fee, scanning every entry each time, so a run grows like requests × accounts × entries: 3.4 s at 100× volume, 96 s at 300×, on only about 6,000 records. Authorization status is also rebuilt from the log per request, so card latency degrades early.

Unbounded state: the log and entries grow forever, holds never expire, and the fee re-check always starts from Day 1.

Cheapest fix: a running total per account per value date, updated on append and rebuildable from the log. The re-check then touches one account from one date onwards. A period close later bounds growth over time.

2. Value-dated entries in production

Day 5 closed at −230.00 with three fees and ended at 465.00. Statements already shown are wrong, the declined Auth-B can't be undone, and past fees and interest must be re-run as new entries. CBUAE consumer protection requires refunding charges caused by the bank's own error immediately, which is why my refunds carry each fee's own date. Every back-dated entry also needs an audit trail.

Control before go-live: maker-checker. An entry dated before its booking day needs a reason code and a second approver. My ledger lacks this; it is the test I left failing on purpose.
